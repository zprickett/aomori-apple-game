## Conventions

- glTF axes: **+Y up, metres, character/object fronts face +Z** (toward camera/customer). Character LEFT = +X, RIGHT = -X.
- Every item is a separate root node at the world origin (upgrades are world-placed). Roots of multi-part items are empty nodes; single-part items are the mesh itself.
- All meshes are flat-shaded (split normals); materials are plain Principled/PBR factors (no textures). Only `Apple_Body` carries a UV map (spherical: u around, v=0 top -> 1 bottom).
- Materials are shared by name across the file; the game can remap/tint by name (`M_AppleSkin`, `M_Jam` are meant to be re-tinted). `M_Glass` is alphaMode BLEND, alpha 0.28. `M_Lantern` is emissive (strength 0.6).
- Animation is node-based (no skins/bones): rotate arm/leg nodes about their shoulder/hip pivots, scale `*_Eyes` on Y to blink, sway `Noren_*` / `Lantern` about their top pivots.
- Arm poses (glTF Euler, radians shown in deg): reach to counter = ArmL/ArmR rotation.x = -70 deg; wave = Chef_ArmR rotation.z = -140 deg (raises the right arm up/outward). Customer legs: rotate .x about the hip for walking.
- Chef is ~1.65 m tall incl. beret (shoulders at 1.11 so her chest shows above the 0.95 counter). Kids 1.05-1.15 m, grandpa ~1.28 m.
